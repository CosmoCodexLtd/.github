# Contributing

Thank you for helping to correct and test our work. This guide applies to every repository in the CosmoCodexLtd organisation that does not have its own.

## What helps most

- **Corrections.** A citation that is wrong, a link that is dead or points to the wrong work, a standard that has been withdrawn or superseded, a step in a proof that does not follow.
- **Reproducibility reports.** A number, table, figure or theorem check that you could not reproduce from the released code, or that you could reproduce on a setup we did not test.
- **Prior work we missed.** A paper or system that already answers, or bears on, one of our [open questions](https://github.com/CosmoCodexLtd#open-questions).

For a security problem, do not open an issue. Follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## How to contribute

1. **Open an issue first** for anything larger than a typo, using one of the issue forms: citation or link error, reproducibility report, or research question. The forms ask for what we need to act.
2. **Cite the source itself.** Link the paper (DOI or arXiv), the specification or the reference implementation, not a summary of it. Give authors, title, venue and year.
3. **Check it yourself before submitting.** Open the link and compare the citation with the publisher's page. If an AI tool helped you, say so, and check every reference against its source.
4. **Mark status.** Say when a standard is a draft or withdrawn, when a paper is a preprint, and when a project is archived.
5. **Keep pull requests small.** One correction or one topic per pull request.

We read every issue and pull request. We are a small team, so replies can take some time.

## Licence of contributions

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it is licensed under the licence of the repository you contribute to, as stated in that repository's README and `LICENSE` file. Do not submit material you do not have the right to license that way.

## Conduct

Everyone taking part is expected to follow the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
