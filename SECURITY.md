# Security policy

This policy covers the websites and published code of CosmoCodex Ltd: cosmocodex.com and the public repositories in this organisation.

## Reporting a problem

Email **info@cosmocodex.com** with "Security" in the subject line.

Please include:

- what is affected (URL, repository or file);
- what you found and how to reproduce it;
- what you think the impact is.

Do not open a public issue for a security problem. Do not send personal data or other people's data in a report; describe where it is exposed instead.

## What to expect

- We read every report and aim to acknowledge it within five working days.
- We will tell you what we intend to do and when the problem is fixed.
- If you would like to be credited, say so in your report.

We do not run a bug bounty and do not pay for reports.

## Testing

Please act in good faith. Do not access, change or delete data that is not yours, do not degrade the service for others, and do not use social engineering or physical attacks. Stop and report as soon as you have enough to show that the problem exists.

## Other products

Social Neuron and TheVPNMatrix publish their own contact details on their own sites. Reports about those products sent to the address above will be passed on.
